I am a Ph.D. researcher in IEMS at the University of Central Florida's Complex Adaptive Systems Lab, advised by Dr. Niloofar Yousefi. My research centers on large language models and agentic AI, with a focus on making them safe and reliable. I study how to monitor tool-using agents and intervene on unsafe actions at runtime, why individually safe models can still fail when composed into multi-agent systems, and how to resolve outdated parametric memory in language models at test time. I also work on generative diffusion for scientific applications, where I build constraint-aware models for molecule generation, and on uncertainty-aware biomedical AI. Before my PhD I spent two years as an AI engineer building LLM and retrieval systems over pharmaceutical literature.

  1. Agentic AI Safety
    • Structured runtime safety and intervention for tool-using LLM agents NEXUS
    • Why safe models do not compose into safe multi-agent systems ChannelGuard
    • Diagnostic reasoning safety benchmark for clinical LLM agents CAREBENCH
  2. Reliable & Trustworthy LLMs
    • Test-time characterization of parametric temporal conflict Temporal Conflict
    • Clinical uncertainty governance for risk-aware decision support MedBayes-Lite
    • Parameter-efficient fine-tuning for language and vision models PEFT A2Z
  3. Generative Diffusion for Science
    • Motif-constrained discrete diffusion with a motif-aware graph-to-sequence tokenizer MoCDiff
    • Risk-optimal role-aware corruption for masked molecular graph diffusion MotifRole-Diff
