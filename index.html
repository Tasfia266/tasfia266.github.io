About

Research

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Industrial Engineering & Management Systems at the University of Central Florida's Complex Adaptive Systems Lab, advised by Dr. Niloofar Yousefi. I'm currently working on multi-objective optimization for generative diffusion models, focused on constrained molecule generation, where I study how to balance competing objectives — validity, novelty, and structural risk — during sampling. I'm also expanding my research into AI safety, reasoning, and alignment for tool-using LLM agents, building on my work on NEXUS, a runtime safety layer that monitors and intervenes on agent behavior before unsafe actions execute. More broadly, I'm interested in trustworthy and biomedical AI: resolving outdated parametric knowledge in language models and building uncertainty-aware systems for clinical decision support. Before my Ph.D. I spent two years as an AI engineer building LLM and retrieval systems over pharmaceutical literature.

  1. Multi-Objective Optimization & Generative Diffusion
    Constrained molecule generation with motif-aware tokenization and role-aware corruption (MoCDiff, MotifRole-Diff).
  2. AI Safety, Reasoning & Alignment
    Runtime monitoring and intervention for tool-using LLM agents; safety under multi-agent composition (NEXUS, ChannelGuard, CAREBENCH).
  3. Trustworthy & Biomedical AI
    Temporal-conflict resolution and uncertainty-aware clinical decision support (Parametric Temporal Conflict, MedBayes-Lite, R-GAT).
